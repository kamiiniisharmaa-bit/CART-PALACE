Where objects carry meaning

CART-PALACE was born from a quiet belief — that the things we surround ourselves with should feel honest, beautiful, and lasting.

Our story

CART-PALACE began as a small studio apartment project in 2025, when our founder, Elara Chen, found herself searching for home goods that didn't scream for attention — but whispered stories of craftsmanship. Dissatisfied with mass-produced trends and disposable decor, she set out to partner with independent artisans, ceramicists, and textile weavers who put soul into every detail.

Today, we've grown into a curated destination for those who seek the mindful and the unique. Every item — from a hand-thrown stoneware mug to a naturally dyed linen cushion — is chosen for its texture, its durability, and the human hand behind it. We don't follow fast fashion cycles; we follow seasons, materials, and makers who respect both.

Our name, Lumina, means "light" — a reminder that well-made objects bring warmth and clarity into everyday life. We're proud to be a slow-commerce space where quality never takes a backseat.

What guides us

Artisan First

We work directly with small-batch makers, ensuring fair wages and authentic craftsmanship in every piece.

Conscious Materials

From reclaimed woods to natural fibers, we prioritize materials that respect the planet and its resources.

Timeless over trendy

We avoid disposable design — our collections are meant to be loved, used, and passed on for years.

Thoughtful Packaging

Minimal, plastic-free, and fully recyclable — because the unboxing should feel as good as the product.
