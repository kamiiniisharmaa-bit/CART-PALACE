Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 5, 2026

Your Privacy Matters to Us

At CART-PALACE ("we," "our," or "us"), we believe that your personal information should be handled with the same care and respect we give to every handcrafted object we sell. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website (www.CART-PALACE.com) or make a purchase. By using our site, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

We are committed to transparency. If you have any questions after reading, please reach out to us at privacy@CART-PALACE.com.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide you with a seamless shopping experience and to improve our offerings. This includes:

Personal Information You Provide

  • Contact & Identity Data: Name, email address, billing/shipping address, phone number.
  • Payment Data: Credit card details or PayPal information (processed securely by our payment partners; we do not store full card numbers).
  • Order History: Products purchased, returns, and preferences.
  • Communications: Any messages you send us via email, chat, or contact forms, including feedback or support requests.

Automatically Collected Data

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, clicks, scroll behavior, and referring website.
  • Device & Browser Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, screen resolution, and language settings.
  • Cookies & Similar Technologies: Small text files stored on your device to remember preferences and enhance functionality (see Section 7).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Order Processing & Fulfillment: To process payments, pack and ship your items, and provide order confirmations and tracking updates.
  • Customer Support: To respond to your inquiries, process returns or exchanges, and resolve any issues.
  • Improving Our Store: To analyze trends, monitor site performance, and enhance product selection and user experience.
  • Marketing Communications (with consent): To send you newsletters, artisan stories, and special offers. You may opt out at any time via the "unsubscribe" link in our emails.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws, tax regulations, or respond to lawful requests from authorities.
Our promise: We never sell your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes. Your trust is the foundation of our brand.

3. Sharing Your Information

We only share your information with trusted third parties who help us operate our business. These partners are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it only for the services they provide to CART-PALACE:

  • Shipping Carriers: USPS, FedEx, or DHL to deliver your order (name and address only).
  • Payment Processors: Stripe and PayPal for secure payment transactions.
  • IT & Analytics Services: Platforms like Shopify (our ecommerce host) and Google Analytics to monitor site performance.
  • Legal Obligations: If required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of CART-PALACE, our customers, or others.

We do not share, rent, or sell your personal data to advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated third parties.

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law (e.g., tax records). For order histories, we typically keep records for 7 years to comply with financial regulations. Marketing preferences and browsing data are retained for up to 24 months unless you request deletion.

If you close your account or request deletion, we will delete or anonymize your data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain certain information for legal or fraud prevention reasons.

5. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location (including residents of the EEA, UK, California, and other regions), you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your personal information, subject to certain legal exceptions.
  • Opt-out of Marketing: Unsubscribe from promotional emails by clicking the link in any email or contacting us.
  • Data Portability: Receive a machine-readable copy of your data (where technically feasible).
  • Do Not Sell My Info: We do not sell personal information as defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), so no opt-out is needed.

To exercise any of these rights, please email privacy@CART-PALACE.com. We will respond within 30 days. We may ask for verification of identity before processing your request.

6. Security of Your Information

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmitted between your browser and our site. Payment card information is tokenized and processed by PCI-DSS compliant partners. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

In the unlikely event of a data breach, we will notify affected users and relevant authorities as required by law.

7. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies (web beacons, pixels) to enhance your browsing experience, remember cart contents, and understand how visitors interact with our site. Cookies are small text files stored on your device. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings, but disabling certain cookies may affect site functionality (e.g., adding items to cart).

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for shopping cart, checkout, and security.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your language and region settings.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us measure traffic and improve our site (e.g., Google Analytics).

We do not use cookies for targeted advertising without your explicit consent (where required by law).

8. Children's Privacy

CART-PALACE does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, we will delete it immediately. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, please contact us.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external sites (e.g., artisan profiles, social media). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third-party websites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

10. International Data Transfers

CART-PALACE is based in the United States. If you are accessing our site from outside the US, please be aware that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the US. We comply with applicable data protection laws, including GDPR for European users. By using our site, you consent to this transfer, subject to appropriate safeguards.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or operational needs. When we make changes, we will revise the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. For significant changes, we may also notify you via email or a notice on our homepage. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
